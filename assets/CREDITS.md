# Asset credits

Every model, texture or picture in the game that **someone else made** gets a line here, even when the licence says credit isn't needed. Things you made yourself can go here too, so there's one list of everything.

Only use assets made by people (photos, scans, hand-made or built by an artist), not AI-generated ones. How to check a licence: [docs/BLENDER.md](../docs/BLENDER.md#6-textures-make-your-own-or-use-ones-youre-allowed-to).
